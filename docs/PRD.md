# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Vision
Aqua is a specialized subdomain of Rural Utility Cost dedicated to providing a water-production economics and decision-support hub for aquaculture, fish farms, ponds, tanks, shellfish, and aquaponics.

## User Personas
1. **Aquaculture Operator**: Needs to quickly calculate feed conversion ratios, startup costs, and overall profit.
2. **Financial Advisor**: Assesses the viability of farming operations using the economics hub.
3. **Student/Researcher**: Uses the tools to study the economics of various aquaculture systems.

## Feature List
- **Cost Estimator**: Estimate upfront capital and variable costs.
- **Feed & FCR Calculator**: Model feed requirements.
- **Harvest & Survival Modeler**: Estimate harvest output based on stocking densities.
- **Profit & Breakeven Tool**: Analyze cycle profitability.
- **System Comparison**: Evaluate extensive vs. intensive systems.
- **Favorites**: Save tools locally for quick access.
